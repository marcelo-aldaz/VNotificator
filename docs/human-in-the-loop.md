# Human-in-the-Loop

Automated rules calculate temporary indicators from course activity and
notification state. They do not establish a diagnosis or make an educational
decision. Authorised staff must review the underlying course context before
contacting a student or recording an intervention.

Required controls:

- display indicators as review priorities, not facts about a student;
- allow teachers to add contextual notes and validation;
- retain who performed each follow-up action;
- allow correction, closure, export, and deletion under institutional policy;
- prohibit automated sanctions, grading changes, or enrolment decisions;
- periodically audit threshold performance and disproportionate effects.
