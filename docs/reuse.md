# Reuse

Other Moodle institutions can reuse VNotificator by adapting threshold values,
notification schedules, role capabilities, section classification terms, and
optional VTutor integration. Research reuse may compare configurable rules,
notification delivery, and human-reviewed follow-up across contexts.

Do not treat institutional thresholds as universal. Report local Moodle
version, cohort definition, observation period, configuration, missing data,
and human validation procedures when publishing results.
